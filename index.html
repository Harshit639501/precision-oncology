How the model works

The live path from account access to an explainable treatment recommendation.

1

Access and readiness

FastAPI loads users, model files, encoders, and the genomic dataset. You sign in to receive a session token.

2

Variant input

Select a gene, chromosome, exon, and transcript position from the available dataset options.

3

XGBoost classification

The API normalizes the position, encodes categorical values, and predicts the cancer probability.

4

Clinical evidence

The response includes confidence, feature impacts, a mutation graph, and gene-position density.

Cancerous probability >= 50%Random Forest ranks drugs, then the page shows the top recommendation, alternatives, and estimated treatment costs with GST.
Cancerous probability < 50%The result is marked benign or normal, no targeted drug is recommended, and monitoring is suggested.
For research and demonstration use. Confirm results with qualified clinical and genomic review.
